## Prompt

Wide 16:9 flat vector illustration, cool muted palette (slate blue, teal, soft amber accent),
generous negative space, 40–60% breathing room, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

Scene: a small self-hosted server room drawn in cross-section on the left. Inside it, a cozy

Outside on the right, a big public signpost at a road fork — two roads leave the frame toward
distant generic cloud shapes.

Center: a toll gate with a single inspector figure at the gate. Above the gate hangs a signpost
with two arrow arms pointing in *contradictory* directions, showing that the same name was
answered twice with different addresses.

Left half of the gate (the older behaviour): a dashed, wobbly road passes the inspector and then
secretly curves back and enters the server room vault through a narrow mouse-hole — quiet, subtle

straight through it, the anchor is chained to a small checklist board held by the inspector, and the
solid road continues outward to the clouds, never touching the room. A clear "blocked at the hole"
detail: the mouse-hole on that side is bricked over.

Mood: calm engineering, no alarm, no fire, no skulls, no padlocks glowing. Simplified silhouettes
only — no realistic human faces. Composition offset slightly left, road lines readable at thumbnail
