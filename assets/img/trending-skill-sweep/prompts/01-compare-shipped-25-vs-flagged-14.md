# Illustration 01: 37 cards funnel down to 25, and 14 of them go dark

## Composition
A hand-drawn three-stage funnel on cream paper, read left to right, no words.

- LEFT STAGE: a loose stack of 37 small note cards, drawn as a slightly messy
  pile with visible edges, grouped into four little clusters of different sizes
  (one tall cluster, one medium, one small, one tiny). Each card has one short
  horizontal dash near its top to suggest a title line, nothing readable.
- MIDDLE STAGE: a hand-drawn gate, drawn as a cheap paper arch with a hand
  crank on top. Two of the four clusters from the left pile never reach the
  gate: they are drawn behind it, on the floor, with a small "off to the side"
  arrow. The cards that pass through come out as a tidier stack.
- RIGHT STAGE: the surviving stack, split into two parts. The larger part
  (fourteen cards) is drawn dim, filled with a soft grey hatch and each carrying
  a small padlock silhouette on its face; the smaller part (eleven cards) is
  drawn bright with plain cream fill. Above the dim part hangs a light bulb
  drawn with a crossed-out filament, meaning "the model will not switch this on".
- BOTTOM: a thin dotted line running from the dim stack to a tiny hand holding a
  pointing stick, meaning "you have to name it yourself".

The three stages must be clearly unequal in visual weight: the left pile is the
busiest, the middle gate is the smallest element, the right split is the most
contrasty.
