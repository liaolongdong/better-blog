# Cover: a trending leaderboard where only one peak has been measured

## Composition
One wide hand-drawn scene, 16:9, no words anywhere in the image.

Twenty-one hills of very different heights sit in a single row across the frame,
drawn like a hand-sketched bar leaderboard on cream paper. They are ranked from
tallest on the left to shortest on the right, with generous gaps and wobbly
outline strokes. Each hill is a small mound of paper with a tiny flag on top.

Three of the leftmost hills (tallest ones) and one hill slightly right of centre
carry the same small hand-drawn symbol on their slope: a jigsaw puzzle piece
outlined in ink, meaning "these belong to the same category". Nine hills in
total carry that puzzle piece, scattered rather than grouped, so the pattern is
visible but not tidy.

The fourth hill from the left — not the tallest — is the only one being worked
on. A small human figure stands on it with a tape measure, unrolled toward the
ground. The figure is drawn simply, back partly to the viewer, no facial detail.
Around the first three hills there is nothing but faint air; their flags are
just waving.

Bottom edge: a soft pencil shelf line, a couple of stray measurement ticks on
the paper, one small coffee ring stain at the right margin.

## ZONES
- LEFT: the tallest three hills with puzzle-piece marks, flags, no figure on them
- CENTER-LEFT: the fourth hill, the figure with the tape measure, the unrolled tape
- RIGHT: the remaining shorter hills, some with puzzle pieces, some without
- BACKGROUND: cream paper texture, faint grid, no horizon detail
