# Illustration prompt — 三个开关，一条时间轴

Purpose: sits inside the 使用示例 section of a Chinese article about `ascii.rest`, directly under a
paragraph reporting three measurements (plays while on screen, holds the first frame when off
screen, holds it when the reader asks for reduced motion). The picture must make those three
readings visible without repeating the sentences.

---

Hand-drawn educational infographic in sketch-notes style on warm cream paper. Wide 16:9. Three
stacked horizontal lanes sharing one hand-inked time axis arrow at the bottom pointing right. Each
lane is a small scenario, drawn as a strip of frames rather than a chart.

Lane 1, tagged `ON SCREEN`: a row of six little rectangular frames, each containing a slightly
different coarse character-mosaic — a hill climbing, a moon rotating, water strokes shifting — so
the mosaic visibly advances left to right. A soft sage pastel block sits behind this lane.

Lane 2, tagged `OFF SCREEN`: only the first frame is drawn with content; the remaining four frames
are identical copies of that same first mosaic, joined by a faint dashed hand-drawn line, and a
small hand-drawn eye with a slash through it hovers over them. Behind it, a dusty slate pastel
block. Halfway along this lane, one frame gets a small solid sage dot under it, meaning "it
resumes when it comes back" — then the last frame flattens again with a tiny pause glyph drawn as
two short vertical strokes.

Lane 3, tagged `REDUCED MOTION`: five identical frames, all the same mosaic as each other, and at
the left end a small hand-drawn toggle switch drawn in the off position with a short curved arrow.
A muted terracotta pastel block sits behind it, paler than the other two.

Labels: ONLY these ASCII strings may appear — `ON SCREEN`, `OFF SCREEN`, `REDUCED MOTION`, and a
single `t` on the axis arrow. No other lettering, no Chinese characters, no numbers, no units, no
watermark, no signature.

Rendering: loose black ink outlines with visible wobble and slightly doubled lines, flat pastel
fills that stop short of the outlines, a little paper grain, nothing glossy, no gradients, no 3D,
no realistic humans or faces. Composition: lanes left-aligned with generous cream margin on the
right, vertical rhythm even, 45–55% whitespace.
