# Illustration 3: 同一条漏洞，两种修法

## Composition
A two-column side-by-side comparison on cream paper, split by a vertical
hand-drawn dashed divider. Left column = the 16 line's fix, right column = the
15 line's fix. Same visual weight on both sides.

## ZONES
- TOP: one short heading above each column, left and right of the divider.
- LEFT ZONE: a slightly tilted paper slip drawn like a config file excerpt. Its
  first line is the clean heading of the slip — no strike, no underline, no arrow
  touching it. The second line contains two version constraints side by side, and
  ONLY the left one (`^0.35.3`) carries a single horizontal strikethrough; a small
  hand-drawn arrow runs from that struck token to the token on its right. Below
  the slip, a simple toggle lever drawn hard-over (binary, no in-between).
- RIGHT ZONE: a magnifying glass hovering over a small round dial/valve with a
  pointer; a thin measurement ruler doodle leans against the dial. Below, a
  simple gate drawn as a small hinged flap that is open.
- BOTTOM CENTER, straddling the divider: one short caption with a small
  double-headed arrow under both columns.

## LABELS
Exact strings, clean Chinese rendering, nothing else written:
- Left heading: "16 线"
- Right heading: "15 线"
- On the left paper slip, first line: "package.json"
- On the left paper slip, second line: "^0.35.3 → ^0.35.4"
- Left caption under the toggle: "版本号硬切"
- Right caption under the gate: "运行时查解码器"
- Small label beside the dial: "heif"
- Bottom caption: "形状不一样"

## COLORS
- Cream paper background (#FDF6E3), faint paper grain
- Black ink (#2B2B2B) for all outlines, divider, lettering
- Soft coral (#FF6B6B) for the single struck token "^0.35.3" and the left
  toggle's base plate
- Mint green (#88D8B0) for the new version string underline and the open gate flap
- Warm yellow (#FFD93D) wash inside the magnifying glass lens

## STYLE
- Hand-drawn sketch-notes: wobbly rules, uneven lettering baseline, dashed
  divider with irregular dash length
- Both columns occupy roughly equal width; no side looks like a winner
- 40%+ empty cream, generous outer margins

## NEGATIVE
No humans, no hands, no code beyond the two lines on the paper slip, no English
sentences, no extra numbers, no 3D, no gradients, no drop shadows, no watermark,
no strikethrough and no underline on the words "package.json".
