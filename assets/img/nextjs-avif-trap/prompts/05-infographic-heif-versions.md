# Illustration 4: 差一个 patch 号，跨过一条线

## Composition
A hand-drawn bar chart on cream paper. Three vertical bars rising from a common
baseline, crossed by one horizontal dashed threshold line near the top. Each bar
carries two labels: the installed sharp version below the baseline, the reported
libheif version at the bar's tip.

## ZONES
- BASELINE: a solid hand-drawn horizontal rule with a small arrowhead at the
  right end, sitting low in the frame.
- BAR 1 (left): the shortest bar, its tip clearly BELOW the dashed threshold
  line, gap visible.
- BAR 2 (center): a tall bar whose tip stops just barely below the dashed line —
  the near-miss is the point of the picture, so leave a thin but real gap.
- BAR 3 (right): a bar whose tip reaches the dashed line and pokes slightly past
  it.
- THRESHOLD: one horizontal dashed line across the whole chart, labelled at its
  right end.
- MARKS: a hand-drawn cross mark floating above bar 1's tip, a second cross mark
  just above bar 2's tip, a check mark above bar 3's tip.
- LEFT EDGE: a faint vertical axis with two tiny tick strokes, unlabelled.

## LABELS
Exact strings, clean rendering, nothing else written:
- Bar 1 below baseline: "sharp 0.34.5"
- Bar 1 at tip: "1.20.2"
- Bar 2 below baseline: "sharp 0.35.3"
- Bar 2 at tip: "1.23.1"
- Bar 3 below baseline: "sharp 0.35.4"
- Bar 3 at tip: "1.23.2"
- Threshold line, right end: "1.23.2"
- Small caption at bottom center: "三次独立安装"

## COLORS
- Cream paper background (#FDF6E3), faint paper grain
- Black ink (#2B2B2B) for baseline, bar outlines, all lettering
