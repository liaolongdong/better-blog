# Illustration 1: 六个日夜的尺子

## Composition
A horizontal hand-drawn timeline across the middle of a cream paper sheet, five
nodes on a slightly wobbly ink line, two span brackets drawn above the line.

## ZONES
- CENTER BAND: a long hand-drawn horizontal axis line with arrowhead at right end.
- FIVE NODES on the axis, left to right, each a small filled dot with a short
  vertical tick and a label block beneath it.
- UPPER LEFT: a tight curly brace spanning node 2 to node 3, very narrow, with a
  small label above it.
- UPPER RIGHT-CENTER: a wide curly brace spanning node 3 to node 4, with a larger
  label above it and a small clock doodle beside that label.
- CORNER (bottom-right): a tiny crossed-out calendar page doodle, decorative only.

## LABELS
Exact Chinese/digit strings, rendered cleanly, no other text anywhere:
- Node 1: "8/25 11:17" on first line, "libheif 公告" on second line
- Node 2: "8/25 14:52" on first line, "关掉 AVIF" on second line
- Node 3: "8/25 16:17" on first line, "v16.3.3 发布" on second line
- Node 4: "8/31 20:02" on first line, "v16.3.4 恢复" on second line
- Node 5: "9/8 21:21" on first line, "公告公开" on second line
- Narrow brace label: "4 分 18 秒"
- Wide brace label: "6 天 3 小时"
Node 3 and node 4 dots are visually farther apart than the others, so the wide
brace reads as the long gap.

## COLORS
- Cream paper background (#FDF6E3), faint paper grain
- Black ink (#2B2B2B) for axis, ticks, all lettering
- Soft coral (#FF6B6B) for node 2 and node 3 dots and the "6 天 3 小时" brace
- Mint green (#88D8B0) for node 4 dot
- Warm yellow (#FFD93D) for the small clock doodle fill

## STYLE
- Hand-drawn sketch-notes: imperfect line weight, slight baseline wobble in the
  lettering, overshoot at the arrowhead
- Labels sit in plain open space, NOT inside boxes or bubbles
- 45%+ empty cream; keep generous margins on all sides

## NEGATIVE
No realistic humans, no screenshots, no 3D, no gradients, no drop shadows, no
extra words, no English sentences, no watermark text, no invented dates or times
beyond the seven labels listed.
