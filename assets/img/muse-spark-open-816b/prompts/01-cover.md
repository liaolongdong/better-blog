# Cover prompt — Muse Spark Open 816B

Core conflict: 816B open weights vs a 1.6TB memory wall. A colossal stack of 65 dark weight shards towers over a tiny Mac Studio on a desk; a narrow doorway at the bottom is marked by a thin measuring line. The shards are heavy, physical, archive-like; the machine is small and warm-lit. The image should say: the weights are public, but the door is narrow.

Composition: 16:9, subject in the vertical middle 40–60%, generous negative space around it, readable at 320px. Editorial collage / paper-and-ink texture, warm cream background, deep ink blue and safety orange accents. Strong silhouette, one clear visual landing point. No text, no logos, no UI chrome. Avoid purple gradients, glowing spheres, isometric cubes, all-pale or all-dark palettes, explosions, warning lights, news-shock styling.
