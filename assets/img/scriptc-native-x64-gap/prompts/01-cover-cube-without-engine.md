# Cover prompt — 一块不带运行时的原生方块

Purpose: cover for a Chinese article about `scriptc`, a TypeScript→native compiler,
and about the one platform pack that is missing on Intel Macs. The cover carries NO
text (the blog template renders the title), so the idea must be purely visual.

Layout: warm off-white paper with faint grain. Left third: a hand-drawn sheet of
source code — loose squiggle lines of different lengths, one or two small angle
brackets, no readable words. Centre: a short, confident ink arrow made of two
rough strokes. Right third: a solid, slightly irregular dark rounded cube (the
standalone executable), drawn with one heavy outline and a soft warm shadow; inside
it, no gears, no interpreter, no engine icon — the emptiness IS the point.

Below and slightly behind the cube: a much larger ghosted outline of a runtime
sphere (the JS engine you would normally have to ship with), drawn in a thin dotted
line at ~25% opacity, so the size contrast reads at a glance.

Bottom-right corner: one small detached tile, drawn in a thin ink outline with a
question-mark-free gap next to it — the sense of "a piece that was never published"
without spelling it out.

Style: hand-drawn sketch-notes, black ink linework with slight wobble, one warm
accent (soft terracotta or amber) used only on the arrow and the cube's shadow,
40–60% breathing room, no gradient mesh, no 3D render, no photorealism, no
realistic humans, no letters, no digits, no logos, no watermark.
