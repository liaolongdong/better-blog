Hand-drawn editorial illustration, warm palette, wide 16:9 composition, no text of any kind.

A long wooden counter, seen slightly from above, with five paper "proof tokens" lying on it in
a row. Each token is a rounded rectangle card with a scalloped top edge like a seal, drawn in
loose black ink with a warm cream fill. Each card shows a DIFFERENT visible tampering mark, so
they read as five separate forgeries rather than five copies: card one has a hasty stitch seam
resewn across its middle; card two has a corner peeled back revealing a darker layer beneath;
card three has a hairline crack filled in with lighter putty; card four has a fresh ink stamp
smudged over an older, scraped-off stamp; card five has its center cut out and a different
small paper patch glued in, with the glue line still visible.

A single small brass hand-stamp on a short articulated arm hangs over the counter from the
upper right, pressed down onto only the first two cards — those two carry a bold round
imprint. The arm cannot reach the other three; they sit further along the counter in slightly
cooler shadow, untouched, their tampering marks still plainly visible.

Behind the counter, a low warm wall with a closed wooden gate and a heavy latch (the boundary
that was supposed to be checked), softly out of focus. Warm late-afternoon light from the left,
long soft shadows, dust grains in the air.

Mood: quiet inspection that misses things. Patient, a little sardonic.

Palette: warm cream and sand paper tones, terracotta and amber accents, muted olive for the
counter wood, thin dark ink outlines, one cool grey-green shadow for the untouched cards.
Flat color shapes with visible paper grain, no gradients except the lamp-less window light.

Composition: the five cards form a horizontal band slightly below center, generous empty warm
space in the upper third for a title overlay (leave it truly empty).

Absolutely no text: no letters, no words, no characters of any language, no numbers, no
digits, no labels, no stamps with glyphs, no logos, no UI, no signage, no watermark, no
signature, no border frame. All imprints must be blank abstract rings, never readable marks.
