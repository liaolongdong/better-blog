# 封面候选（FLUX 3 Klein 开放权重）

核心冲突：一个 5.6B 的小模型，产出原生 4K 图像，4K 单价只有同厂旗舰的约 1/17。
封面不写中文，默认无字；主体落在垂直中央 40%–60%；四周留白；320px 仍能看清。
禁止：紫色渐变、发光球体、等距立方体、全 pale / 全 dark、新闻震惊体（大红大黄、爆炸、警灯）。

## 候选 A（首选构图）

Prompt:
Editorial still-life photograph on a warm off-white paper background. A single tiny matte-black AI chip, no larger than a thumbnail, sits slightly left of center. It casts a large, sharp, detailed shadow shaped like a 4K photograph with visible fine texture, filling the right half of the frame. The shadow is crisp at the edges and subtly gradients from dark ink to warm gray. One thin red price line descends from the large image shadow to a small blank price tag beside the chip. Overhead studio lighting, soft directional light, generous negative space at top and bottom, muted warm palette (paper white, ink black, one accent red), high contrast, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no purple, no glowing spheres, no isometric cubes, no people. The small chip and the large image shadow are both fully inside the vertical middle 40–60% band, readable as a silhouette at 320px.

## 候选 B（备选构图）

Prompt:
Minimal editorial still-life on a cool light gray background. On the left, a neat stack of one hundred small square photo prints; on the right, a single large 4K photo print standing upright. A thin red line runs from the large print to a small red price tag, and a second thin red line runs from the stack to a much smaller price tag. The visual contrast is "many small prints vs one large print" and "big price vs small price". Overhead view, soft studio light, generous negative space, muted palette (light gray, charcoal, one accent red), no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no purple, no glowing spheres, no isometric cubes. Main objects centered in the vertical 40–60% band, readable at 320px.

## 选择标准

- 320px 缩略图里，第一眼能否看出“小模型 / 大输出”或“大图价格骤降”的冲突。
- 主体是否在垂直中间带，上下是否平衡。
- 是否是“一张白图”或“横带挤满小字”。
- 是否避开了紫色渐变、发光球体、等距立方体三件套。
