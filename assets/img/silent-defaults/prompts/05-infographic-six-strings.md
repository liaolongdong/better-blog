# Illustration 4: 那个页面上能看见的全部文字

## Composition
A hand-drawn infographic on cream paper, 16:9, in three parts arranged
left-to-right: a large browser frame occupying the left 55%, a narrow tally
column in the middle, and a count panel on the right. One heading across the
top, one caption across the bottom. 35% of the canvas stays empty cream.

## ZONES
- TOP: one heading line, centred.
- LEFT: a simple hand-drawn browser window — one rounded rectangle with a bar
  across its top containing three small circles (window dots) and a blank
  address pill with no text in it. Inside the window body sit exactly six
  stacked text rows, evenly spaced, left-aligned, each drawn as plain handwriting
  with no bullet, no box, no underline.
- MIDDLE: a vertical tally column headed by a short label. Under the heading,
  seven short hand-drawn link ticks stacked in a column (each tick is a tiny
  chain-link glyph: two small overlapping ovals). Beside the fourth tick only,
  one short path string is written.
- RIGHT: a count panel. A very large coral numeral zero dominates it. To the
  zero's left, a short arrow points at the zero from a dashed-outline box that
  holds one label. Below the zero, a small grey note line.
- BOTTOM CENTRE: one caption line with a short mint-green double underline.

## LABELS
Exact strings, clean Chinese rendering, nothing else written anywhere.
The six rows inside the browser window, in this order, verbatim and in English:
- "Login - Tailwind Plus"
- "Log in to your Tailwind Plus account"
- "Email"
- "Password"
- "Forgot password?"
- "Sign in to account"
Other strings:
- Heading: "那个页面上能看见的全部文字"
- Middle column heading: "7 个 href"
- The single path beside the fourth link tick: "/plus/password/reset"
- Dashed box on the right: "通向那篇公告的链接"
- Small grey note under the zero: "6 串可见文字"
- Caption: "找不到一条路通向那篇文章"

The six window rows must be the ONLY text inside the browser frame. The address
pill must stay empty. The other six link ticks must carry no text.

## COLORS
- Cream paper background #FDF6E3 with faint paper grain and one soft
  coffee-stain smudge in a far corner
- Dark ink #2B2B2B for the frame, the six rows, headings and caption
- Soft coral #FF6B6B for the large zero and its arrow
- Mint green #88D8B0 for the caption double underline
- Muted grey #B8B2A7 for the link ticks, the dashed box outline and the note line
- Warm yellow #FFD93D as a faint marker sweep behind the middle column heading

## STYLE
- Hand-drawn sketch-notes: imperfect linework, visible wobble, small corner
  overshoots, no straightedge feel
- Marker-pen fills with visible stroke direction, never gradients
- The large zero must be drawn by hand, slightly open at the top — not a
  typed glyph
- Generous negative space; nothing touching the outer 6% margin

## NEGATIVE
No logos, no Tailwind branding, no real screenshots, no price numbers, no
download counts, no percentages, no realistic humans, no hands, no faces, no
drop shadows, no 3D render, no photorealism, no watermarks. No text anywhere
except the LISTED strings.
