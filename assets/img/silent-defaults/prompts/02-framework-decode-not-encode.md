# Illustration 1: 同一份配置，四种结果

## Composition
A hand-drawn 2×2 matrix on cream paper, 16:9. One heading strip at the top, a
condition chip at the upper-left, column headers above the two right-hand
cells' columns, row headers left of the two rows, four cells of equal size
drawn as slightly wobbly rounded rectangles. The bottom-right cell is circled
in coral. 40–45% of the canvas stays empty cream.

## ZONES
- TOP: one heading line, centred.
- UPPER LEFT: a small pill/chip drawn like a config line, holding the condition
  text. It sits above the row headers, not inside the grid.
- GRID: two columns × two rows. Column headers sit above the grid, one per
  column. Row headers sit left of the grid, one per row, right-aligned toward
  their row.
- Cell contents: one large numeral group per cell, plus a tiny status mark in
  that cell's lower-right corner (a check tick, or a small tag).
- BOTTOM: one caption line with a short underline, centred under the grid.

## LABELS
Exact strings, clean Chinese rendering, nothing else written anywhere:
- Heading: "同一份配置，四种结果"
- Condition chip: "formats 已含 avif"
- Column header left: "16.3.3"
- Column header right: "16.3.4"
- Row header top: "PNG 源图"
- Row header bottom: "AVIF 源图"
- Cell (PNG 源图 × 16.3.3): "13,504"  with a check tick
- Cell (PNG 源图 × 16.3.4): "13,504"  with a check tick
- Cell (AVIF 源图 × 16.3.4): "6,906"  with a small tag "重编码"
- Cell (AVIF 源图 × 16.3.3): "28,112" with a small tag "原图直出"
- Caption: "编码没关，关的是解码"

The two "13,504" cells must be drawn with identical numerals so the reader can
see they match. The bottom-right cell ("28,112") carries a single imperfect
hand-drawn coral ring around the whole cell.

## COLORS
- Cream paper background #FDF6E3 with faint paper grain
- Dark ink #2B2B2B for grid lines, headers, numerals
- Soft coral #FF6B6B for the ring around the bottom-right cell and its tag
- Mint green #88D8B0 for the two check ticks only
- Warm yellow #FFD93D as a light marker sweep behind the caption underline
- Muted grey #B8B2A7 for the condition chip outline

## STYLE
- Hand-drawn sketch-notes: imperfect linework, visible wobble, small corner
  overshoots, no straightedge feel
- Marker-pen fills with visible stroke direction, never gradients
- Generous negative space; nothing touching the outer 6% margin

## NEGATIVE
No logos, no UI screenshots, no browser chrome, no realistic humans, no faces,
no drop shadows, no 3D render, no photorealism, no watermarks. Do not invent
any additional numbers, units, or labels beyond the LISTED strings — in
particular do not write "bytes", "KB", "ms", or any percentage anywhere.
