# Illustration 3: 三种写法，三种暴露

## Composition
A three-column side-by-side comparison on cream paper, 16:9. Two vertical
hand-drawn dashed dividers split the canvas into three equal lanes. Each lane
has: a heading at the top, one central object, one short line under the object,
and a status mark at the bottom of the lane. The right lane is visually
heavier — its object is drawn larger and its status mark is coral. 40% of the
canvas stays empty cream.

## ZONES
- TOP: one heading line centred above all three lanes.
- LANE 1 (left): heading text; below it a closed padlock resting on top of a
  sheet of paper whose body is drawn as four short ruled strokes (no writing);
  below the sheet one short line; at the lane bottom a mint-green check tick.
- LANE 2 (middle): heading text; below it a large hand-drawn caret symbol
  followed by a version range drawn as a short horizontal bracket with a dot at
  each end; below that one short line; at the lane bottom a mint-green check
  tick.
- LANE 3 (right, larger): heading text; below it a box drawn like a shipping
  crate with an arrow exiting its right side and pointing down into an open
  funnel; a small tag hangs from the arrow. Below the funnel one short line; at
  the lane bottom a coral cross mark.
- BOTTOM CENTRE, straddling the middle divider: one caption line.

## LABELS
Exact strings, clean Chinese rendering, nothing else written anywhere:
- Heading: "三种写法，三种暴露"
- Lane 1 heading: "有 lockfile"
- Lane 1 line: "装锁住的那版"
- Lane 2 heading: "写 ^4.0.0"
- Lane 2 line: "跨不到 5.x"
- Lane 3 heading: "latest · * · npx"
- Lane 3 crate label: "wrangler"
- Lane 3 tag on the arrow: "5.x alpha"
- Lane 3 line: "它替你钉死了"
- Caption: "第三种没有选择权"

## COLORS
- Cream paper background #FDF6E3 with faint paper grain
- Dark ink #2B2B2B for all outlines, arrows and text
- Mint green #88D8B0 for the two check ticks only
- Soft coral #FF6B6B for the cross mark in lane 3 and its funnel outline
- Warm yellow #FFD93D as a light marker sweep behind the crate label
- Muted grey #B8B2A7 for the ruled strokes on lane 1's paper sheet

## STYLE
- Hand-drawn sketch-notes: imperfect linework, visible wobble, small corner
  overshoots, no straightedge feel
- Marker-pen fills with visible stroke direction, never gradients
- The three lanes must read as equal weight except lane 3, which is drawn
  roughly 15% larger
- Generous negative space; nothing touching the outer 6% margin

## NEGATIVE
No logos, no npm or Cloudflare branding, no UI screenshots, no terminal
windows, no realistic humans, no hands, no faces, no drop shadows, no 3D
render, no photorealism, no watermarks. Do not invent package counts, dates or
version numbers beyond the LISTED strings.
