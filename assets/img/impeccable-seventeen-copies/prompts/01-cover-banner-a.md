Flat vector conceptual illustration, 16:9 landscape, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos,
NO watermark, NO signature.

Ground: warm bone / off-white paper (#F2EDE6) filling the whole canvas, with a very subtle
horizontal band across the middle 55% of the height where all action happens; top and bottom
thirds stay nearly empty paper with faint grain.

Composition, left to right inside the middle band:
1. On the far left, ONE document card standing slightly forward of the rest: dark ink-navy (#1D2733)
   rounded rectangle with three short light-grey content lines and one amber (#D97706) line at its
   left edge (a thin vertical accent stripe, 4px wide, clearly visible).
2. To its right, a row of SIXTEEN identical document cards in tight grid, two rows of eight, drawn
   in flat muted slate (#8A98A8) outlines on the bone ground, all perfectly aligned, all with the
   same thin amber left-edge stripe already copied onto them.
3. A single clean curved arrow leaves the first card, sweeps above the grid and comes back down into
   the middle of the grid; the arrow is solid amber, uniform stroke width, one arrowhead, no gradient.

The story the image must tell on its own: one file was edited and the change is being copied across
a whole wall of identical copies.

Style: restrained editorial flat-vector, geometric, generous negative space, no perspective tricks,
no shadows except a 2px flat offset under the first card. Strictly forbidden: purple or violet
gradients, glowing spheres, isometric tech cubes, neon cyan on dark, lens flare, 3D renders,
photographic elements, busy backgrounds, any text or glyph.
