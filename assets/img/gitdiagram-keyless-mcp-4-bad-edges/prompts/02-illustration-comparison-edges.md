A 4:3 hand-drawn conceptual diagram illustration, cool palette, clean paper background, absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numbers, no labels, no legend, no signature, no watermark, no border.

Composition: thirteen to sixteen rounded rectangles (nodes) arranged in four loose clusters on a sheet of paper. Style: soft hand-drawn ink linework with slight wobble, flat muted colors, cool grey paper, slate blue boxes, one amber accent on the solid connectors, pale rose for the four floating dashed ones. Airy, 40–50% whitespace, slight isometric feel.
