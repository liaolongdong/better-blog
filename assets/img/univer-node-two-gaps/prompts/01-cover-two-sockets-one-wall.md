# Cover prompt — 一套内核，两个插座，一道矮墙

Purpose: cover for a Chinese article about `dream-num/univer`, a spreadsheet/document
kernel whose one Facade API runs both in a browser and headless inside a Node process.
The cover carries NO text at all (the blog template renders the title), so the idea
must read purely visually: one kernel, two identical sockets, and a low wall it hits.

Layout: cool off-white paper with faint grain, 40–60% breathing room.
Centre: a small hand-drawn workbook — a rounded rectangle filled with a light
spreadsheet grid (thin even rows and columns), drawn in one confident ink outline
with slight wobble. This single grid is the only "engine" in the picture.

Two sockets, one shape each, both plugged into that same grid by two cables of
identical thickness and identical plug heads — the point is that the connector is
the same on both sides:
- upper-left: a minimal browser-window silhouette (a rounded rectangle with one bar
  across the top and three small dots), drawn thin and ghosted at ~45% opacity.
- lower-right: a minimal terminal/server silhouette (a rounded rectangle with one
  prompt chevron shape, no glyphs), drawn with the same thin ink line at full opacity.

Bottom edge of the grid: the lowest few row lines dissolve into a short dotted line,
and at exactly that dissolve sits a small solid low brick wall segment (two or three
stacked blocks, filled dark with the accent colour). It blocks only the bottom of the
grid, not the whole picture — "a default ceiling you run into", not "it is broken".

Style: hand-drawn sketch-notes, black ink linework with slight wobble, one cool
accent (slate blue) used on the cables and the browser silhouette, one warm accent
(muted amber) reserved ONLY for the low wall blocks. Flat, no gradient mesh, no 3D
render, no photorealism, no realistic humans, no robots, no letters, no digits, no
words, no logos, no watermark, no signature, no border frame.
