# 封面 prompt（16:9，无字）

## 核心冲突
79 个 Agent 名字 vs 磁盘上只有 3 条路径。画面要让人一眼看到“很多条线，只有三条真正落地”。

## 候选 A（首选）
Editorial infographic on a light warm-gray paper background (#F2F0EB), subtle fine grid, generous negative space. On the left, one small dark ink folder/file icon at vertical center. From it, 79 thin ink lines fan out to the right. Exactly 3 lines terminate in solid deep-blue folder tabs; the other 76 lines fade into pale dashed gray and stop in mid-air before reaching anything. A single signal-red circle lightly hand-drawn around the 3 solid folder tabs. Flat vector, crisp black lines, risograph/editorial print feel, no gradients, no glow, no 3D. Subject occupies the vertical 40%–60% band, with breathing room top and bottom and an open exit on the right. Readable at 320px: the contrast between 3 solid paths and a cloud of faded paths must survive.

## 候选 B（备选）
A wide overhead routing board on off-white paper: 79 small square sockets arranged in a loose grid on the left half, only 3 sockets connected by thick black cables to 3 solid folder blocks on the right; the rest of the sockets have short dead-end stubs. One red dot marks the live path. Minimal editorial diagram, flat ink, paper texture, lots of white space, subject centered vertically, no text, no purple, no glowing spheres, no isometric cubes.

## 禁止
紫色渐变、发光球体、等距立方体、全 pale/全 dark、stock 科技图、水印、任何文字。
